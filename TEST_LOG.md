## The LLM quota

- After about eight agent runs in half an hour, the default model returned: "You have reached your trial limit for this model… wait 8m49s for your trial quota to be refilled."
- Retries 10 and 20 minutes later were still refused, each time naming a wait of five to nine minutes. I do not know whether a refused attempt extends the wait.
- Two other managed models answered at once. So the limit is per model.
- Total used on this project today: about 1.3 million tokens across eleven agent runs, plus the workflow run, which I did not measure.
